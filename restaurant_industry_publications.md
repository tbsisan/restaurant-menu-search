# Restaurant Industry Publications & Marketing Channels

A guide to the trade publications, newsletters, and marketing channels read by restaurant owners, operators, and managers (restaurateurs) to help shape B2B marketing strategies.

---

## 1. The Legacy Giants (Authority & Credibility)
Use these platforms for PR campaigns, guest editorials, or traditional B2B display advertising. They target mid-to-large operators and decision-makers.

* **[Nation's Restaurant News (NRN)](https://nrn.com):** The "Wall Street Journal" of the food industry. Covers industry news, trend data, menu innovations, and mergers and acquisitions.
* **[Restaurant Business](https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com):** Highly analytical, data-driven publication focusing on growth strategies, entrepreneurship, technology, and operations.
* **[FSR Magazine](https://www.fsrmagazine.com):** Tailored specifically for Full-Service Restaurant owners and operators.
* **[QSR Magazine](https://www.qsrmagazine.com):** The definitive guide for the Quick-Service and Fast-Casual restaurant segments.

---

## 2. Modern Restaurant Tech Hubs (High-Engagement Blogs)
Tech-forward independent restaurateurs frequently read content hosted by major restaurant software platforms. These blogs offer highly practical marketing and operations advice.

* **[Toast's Restaurant Resources](https://pos.toasttab.com/blog):** Toast publishes industry data reports, trend guides, and marketing templates read by hundreds of thousands of independent operators.
* **[SevenRooms Blog](https://sevenrooms.com/en/blog/):** Focuses heavily on customer CRM, guest retention strategies, reservation booking, and direct-to-consumer marketing.
* **[BentoBox Blog](https://www.getbento.com/blog/):** Offers tactical tips for website design, SEO, digital marketing, and direct online ordering.
* **[Lunchbox Content & Zines](https://lunchbox.io):** A modern direct-ordering platform known for its bold, creative, and edgy B2B campaigns and print/digital zines.

---

## 3. Culture & Competitor Intel (Where Owners Hang Out)
Restaurateurs closely monitor food culture and competitor news. Sponsoring local editions or placement here reaches them during daily leisure reading.

* **[Eater](https://eater.com) (and Eater City Sites):** While consumer-facing, chefs and independent operators read Eater daily to track trends and local news.
* **[Resy OS](https://resy.com/resy-os):** Resy's B2B hub, featuring in-depth profiles on successful restaurateurs, dining room analytics, and reservation trends.

---

## 4. Daily Curated Digests (Efficient B2B Outreach)
* **[Restaurant SmartBrief](https://www.smartbrief.com/industry/foodservice-and-hospitality/restaurants):** A daily email newsletter aggregating top news across the industry. Highly popular with busy owners who want quick updates in their inbox.
