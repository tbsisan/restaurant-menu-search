# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Local diners who know the specific dish they want to buy, crave-driven eaters, and price-sensitive pickup customers in Southgate/Metro Detroit. They want to find and order their exact craving quickly without delivery app markups or navigating fragmented restaurant sites.

## Product Purpose
A dish-level local menu search engine that lets users search for specific foods nearby, choose a restaurant, and place a pickup order at normal in-store prices via an AI phone agent. It aims to bypass delivery-app markup rails and keep prices fair for both diners and mom-and-pop restaurants.

## Brand Personality
Utility & Community. Simple, honest, community-first, and extremely fast. Cozy but tool-like; modern sensibility applied to a Craigslist or Hacker News style layout. Focuses on speed and utility with no corporate fluff.

## Anti-references
Dry yellow-pages directory listings. Over-designed corporate delivery apps (heavy marketing illustrations, generic card layouts, dark patterns, and bloated interfaces).

## Design Principles
- **Craving-First Utility**: The interface centers on the search query and the dish results, not restaurant branding or general cuisine cards.
- **Radical Simplicity & Speed**: Zero visual bloat. Extremely fast interactions, clean structure, and highly functional layouts that respect the user's time.
- **Mom & Pop Honesty**: Clear pricing, 100% restaurant take, explicit source confidence, and a humble, community-focused tone that highlights local merchants instead of corporate portals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast typography for readability under varying outdoor/ambient light.
- Respect user preference for reduced motion.
- Fast page load times and lightweight footprint for low-bandwidth mobile devices.
